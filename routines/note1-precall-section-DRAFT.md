## PART A — EVERY BOOKED STRATEGY CALL (PRE-CALL NOTE 1) — steps 2–4

2. VERDICT = the closer's temperature read for the triage call (S.P.E.E.D. Step 4), one gate + one action:
   • **HOT** — invest strong, start strong, sole decider, Instagram verified, Offer Breakdown video watched (tag/reply "WATCHED"), no math gap. Action: "run the triage call as a payment call — if aligned, take the deposit on this call".
   • **CONFIRM** — good fit, nothing weak, but the video is not confirmed watched. Action: "confirm the slot on the call, then send WhatsApp 1–3 (video + meeting link)".
   • **TRIAGE** — any ONE weak signal: investment weak or vague, start weak, decider weak or blank, low urgency, answers flipped after a disqualification, numbers that don't add up, no application on file, or a HARD CAP. Action: name the exact qualifier question that settles it (money, authority, urgency or video).
   • **RED FLAGS** — two or more weak signals, or Junk/No IG. Action: "conditional confirm — send the two resources, reply by <time> or the slot is cancelled; no reply → free community link, chapter closed". Never say "dial" for Junk — junk leads are not dialed.
   Show the signals and caps in the VERDICT line as today (e.g. "invest: yes, start: this week, sole DM: yes, age 34").

3. Content, in this order (same layout rules, 30-second read):
   TITLE: PRE-CALL NOTE 1 • AI GRADE: Grade — Name
   VERDICT: HOT / CONFIRM / TRIAGE / RED FLAGS — gate + single action
   🔗 links (WhatsApp • Instagram • link in bio • site)
   📅 Day DD Mon, H:MM AM/PM IST • closer • calendar (source) • booking/reschedule history • application count • call status (new / confirmed / WATCHED / READY)
   FORM (DD Mon) — Who / Numbers / Pain + money, newest submission only. Lead with the #1 struggle verbatim; it drives the script.
   BUCKET — one line: **B2C** (not online yet / no online clients) → the call is about why they want to go online • **B2B** (already selling online) → the call is about quality leads and charging more. Say which bucket and why.
   HISTORY — only if old data exists (unchanged)
   RISK FLAGS — only if present, bold labels (unchanged)
   WEB RESEARCH — unchanged (Instagram main + extra accounts, offer, link in bio, site)
   ACTIVITY — unchanged, PLUS the pre-call status: Offer Breakdown video watched? (tag/"WATCHED" reply/"READY" reply/unknown) • which WhatsApp messages (1–5) are already sent • replied to us or not
   TRIAGE SCRIPT — replaces ASK THESE. Fill the Playbook script with the prospect's own words and numbers so the closer reads it off the note. Each line is one bullet, max 10 bullets:
     • **Opener:** "Hey <first name>, how's it going? I'm <closer>, Business Consultant at Independent Online Coach from Aakash's team. You booked a call for <Day> <time>, right?" — add "(switch to Hindi if they reply in Hindi)" when the form or Instagram is Hindi.
     • **Pain:** "In the form you said your biggest challenge is '<#1 struggle verbatim>', correct?" then the two pain-sequence questions that fit their answer best (how long • worst part • what bothers you most • after-effects on the business • how it shows up personally • what if this continues 3–4 months).
     • **Urgency:** one consequence question built on their goal/deadline (e.g. "you want ₹3L/month by Jan — what happens if the leads stay junk till then?") then "So fixing this is a top priority right now, correct?"
     • **Why us:** "What made you decide to book a call with us?" — note what they should mention (our client results, which case study they likely saw from the ad).
     • **Video:** if WATCHED: "Which program are you more inclined towards — any questions on the Offer Breakdown?" • if not: "Have you gone through the videos explaining how we help coaches scale to 3–10L/month? Any reason? No worries, I'll share it on WhatsApp — watch it before our call."
     • **Money:** "For full transparency, the minimum investment to work with us is ₹1L. Are you comfortable investing that in your growth?" — then the branch that fits THEIR form answer: invest Yes → skip ahead • "manage the funds"/"maybe" → "How much have you set aside to grow the business?" • "not in a position" → "glad we figured out the fit — here's the free resource" (7 Secrets to 7 Figures) and end the call.
     • **Authority:** sole decision-maker Yes → confirm in one line • blank or No → "Who's involved in the decision — is it permission or respect? Get their buy-in before we connect."
     • **Contradiction (if any):** one question on the biggest flip or math gap, in their numbers ("you wrote 50 clients at ₹30k but ₹50k–1L a month — which is right?").
     • **Close of triage:** "You seem like a good fit — watch the resource by this evening and reply WATCHED on WhatsApp so I can confirm your <time> call."
   CALL IT — one line, by the Playbook price list and REAL monthly revenue (clients × price ÷ 3 when the band disagrees; flag **Math gap:**):
     • <₹50k or B2C → **Sales Jumpstart (DIH) ₹1L + GST = ₹1.18L**
     • ₹50k–2L → **Sales Accelerator (DFY) ₹3L + GST = ₹3.54L** (default pitch)
     • ≥₹2L with proof, strong investment and sole decider → **Sales Multiplier (DFY Scale) ₹6L + GST = ₹7.08L — founder must be on the call before this pitch**
     • Can't take sales calls themselves → Accelerator with the sales team built and trained by us, not Jumpstart.
     Payment line, always: "upfront first; at most two parts; no payment plans; minimum ₹10k onboarding deposit on the call; ALL IN upgrade only if they clear the pending amount within 24 h." Stall fallback: the free community, never Kickstarter, never a case study before the video.
   WHATSAPP — which Playbook messages to send and when: 1 (intro) + 2 (Power Funnel System + DFY Offer Stack, reply WATCHED) right after the triage call • 3 (meeting link + join rules, no-show = no reschedule) after WATCHED • 4 (Aakash 30L/M, Blue Pond ads, Premium Selling case studies) that night, only after WATCHED • 5 (good-morning, reply READY) on call day. Mark any already sent.
